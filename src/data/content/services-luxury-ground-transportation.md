## The Day Between the Flights

Most executive trips spend more time on the ground than in the air. Hotel to office. Office to lunch. Lunch to a deposition. Deposition to dinner. Dinner to a private event. Event to the residence. The chauffeur is the same person all day, and by the third stop, the principal is not thinking about the car. That is the goal.

Luxury ground transportation from Starter Limo Co is built for that day. Black car service with full-time professional chauffeurs, late-model vehicles, and live dispatch around the clock. Same operations team that runs our private aviation transfers, drawing on twenty-five-plus years of experience as the Official Luxury Chauffeured Partner of the strategic industry partnership.

## How It Works

### As-Directed or Point-to-Point

Two booking models cover most trips. Point-to-point reservations are the simplest. Pickup address, drop-off address, time, and any stops along the way. As-directed bookings hold the chauffeur and vehicle for a block of time, typically four hours or more, while the principal moves through a series of meetings, meals, and events. The chauffeur stays available between stops without re-dispatching.

### Live Dispatch and Routing

Dispatch handles routing changes in real time. If the meeting runs long and dinner shifts from 7:30 to 8:15, the chauffeur stays in position and the next pickup is adjusted. If the principal decides at 4 PM to add a stop on the way, dispatch routes it without anyone calling. The chauffeur drives, dispatch coordinates, and the principal does not manage the trip.

### Account-Based Billing

For active corporate accounts, billing runs centrally. One monthly invoice, one point of contact for questions, and reporting that ties trips back to cost centers, projects, or named executives. Personal trips can be billed separately to the principal if the company prefers.
