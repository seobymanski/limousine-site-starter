## How It Works

### Booking and Confirmation

Call 555.555.5555, email reservations@example.com, or book through your corporate account portal. Provide the tail number, FBO, ETA, passenger count, bag count, and final destination. Confirmation comes back with the chauffeur's name, vehicle type, and contact number, usually within the hour. For active corporate accounts, most of this is already on file.

### Flight Tracking and ETA Updates

Once the trip is on the books, dispatch tracks the tail number in real time. API integrations with corporate aviation systems flow schedule changes into our software automatically. If the jet pushes from a 5 PM departure to 6:15, the chauffeur is repositioned without anyone calling.

### Ramp Pickup

When the FBO allows ramp access, the vehicle pulls up next to the aircraft. The chauffeur handles bags directly off the jet. When the FBO requires curbside, the vehicle stages at the FBO entrance and the chauffeur meets the principal inside. Either way, the principal does not wait.

### Departure and Drop-Off

Direct route to the destination, with traffic handled by dispatch. The chauffeur stays available for the next leg if the trip continues, or signs off cleanly if the day ends at the residence.
