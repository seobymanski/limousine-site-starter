## How It Works

### Booking and Account Setup

Discreet VIP work is typically run through an account, not a one-off booking. Talent agencies, family offices, security firms, and corporate accounts with regular high-profile travel set up the account in advance. The account record holds the principal's preferences, NDA on file, vehicle preferences, route preferences, and any specific chauffeurs the principal has worked with before. Most active discreet accounts have a short list of preferred chauffeurs by name.

### NDA-Compliant Chauffeurs

Every chauffeur assigned to discreet VIP work has an NDA on file with Starter Limo Co. Full-time W-2 employees, not 1099 contractors, which means the NDA actually has weight. Chauffeurs assigned to recurring principals build trust over time. The same chauffeur drives the same principal often enough that the relationship gets simple, which is the whole point.

### Vehicle Selection

Discreet trips often use vehicles that draw less attention than a fully blacked-out SUV with limousine plates. Standard color profiles, less aggressive aftermarket tinting, no obvious livery. Where higher visibility is appropriate, the standard executive black SUV or sedan is the call. The choice depends on the principal, the venue, and the city. Dispatch flags vehicle preferences on every account.

### Advance Work and Route Planning

For high-profile arrivals, advance work matters. The chauffeur knows which entrance to use at the hotel, which side of the FBO has the quieter pull-out, and which approach to the venue avoids the photographers. For trips with private security teams, our chauffeur coordinates with the security lead before the principal arrives. For trips without security, the chauffeur is the front line.

## Common Scenarios

**Talent flying into KOPF for a Miami appearance.** Pickup at Fontainebleau Aviation, vehicle staged on the ramp before wheels-down, drive directly to a hotel back-of-house entrance the chauffeur has confirmed with security in advance.

**Executive arriving at KTEB for a sensitive deal close, security lead on board.** Chauffeur briefed by security in advance, vehicle selected to match security protocol, route pre-walked, no booking platform record, billed under the law firm's master account.

**Sports principal in town for a media obligation.** Sedan with standard tinting (not visibly modified), pickup outside the hotel away from the lobby cameras, drop-off at a side door at the broadcast venue.

**Family office principal moving between two private events on the same evening.** As-directed booking, same chauffeur all evening, standby outside each venue without drawing attention, residence drop-off at the end.
