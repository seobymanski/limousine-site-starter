## Built for the Way Private Aviation Actually Works

Most ground transportation companies offer the same four services. Starter Limo Co does too. The difference is in how they are run. Live dispatch around the clock. Full-time chauffeurs, not contractors. Real-time flight tracking and API integrations with corporate aviation systems. The same standard at KTEB, KOPF, KDAL, and across more than 1,100 cities through the vetted affiliate network. As the Official Luxury Chauffeured Partner of the strategic industry partnership, our service standard is set by the most demanding flight departments in the country.

## Private FBO Transfers

The flagship service. Tail-number to front door, with the chauffeur on the ramp before wheels-down whenever the FBO allows it. We coordinate directly with FBO operators including Meridian Teterboro, Signature, Atlantic Aviation, Jet Aviation, Million Air, Fontainebleau Aviation, and Business Jet Center. Real-time flight tracking means dispatch sees ETA changes the moment they happen.

For the principal, the experience is simple. Step off the jet, into the vehicle, and gone. No standing on the tarmac wondering where the car is. For the flight department, the experience is also simple. One booking, one confirmation, one bill at the end of the month, and a chauffeur who knows what an FBO coordinator's "ready in five" actually means.

## Luxury Ground Transportation

Beyond the airport. Black car coverage between meetings, dinners, hotels, evening events, and the next FBO. Sedans, SUVs, and Sprinters with full-time professional chauffeurs who know how to wait quietly, drive cleanly, and stay out of the way until needed.

This is the service that runs the executive's day. Hotel to office. Office to lunch. Lunch to the next office. Office to dinner. Dinner to the residence. The chauffeur is the same person all day, the vehicle stays clean, and the dispatch desk handles routing changes without bothering the principal.

[Read more about Luxury Ground Transportation](/services/luxury-ground-transportation).

## Charter Bus Service

Coach transport for corporate events, conferences, road shows, and large group movements. Newer motorcoach equipment with the same dispatch discipline that runs the black car fleet. Coaches range from 30-passenger executive shuttles to full 56-passenger highway coaches, with ADA-compliant configurations available.

Common use cases include conference shuttles between hotel and venue, corporate offsites with multiple stops, road shows requiring tight timing, and team movements that combine principal sedans, crew Sprinters, and group coaches in one coordinated package. One account manager runs the full move so the planner has a single point of contact.

[Read more about Charter Bus Service](/services/charter-bus-service).

## Discreet VIP Transportation

Privacy-first ground transport for talent, athletes, UHNW principals, and security-conscious executives. NDAs, advance work, route planning, and chauffeurs trained on protective practices. Vehicles selected to draw less attention, not more.

The service is built around two ideas. First, the principal should never have to manage the trip. Second, no one outside the trip should know it is happening. Pickups timed to avoid attention. Routes pre-walked when the situation calls for it. Coordination with private security teams when present. Standard practice for our clients in entertainment, sports, finance, and family office environments.
