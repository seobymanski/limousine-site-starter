## Group Transport, Built for Corporate Standards

A 200-person investor day in Manhattan needs four coaches running between the hotel and the venue every 20 minutes for two hours. A pharma road show in Dallas needs two executive shuttles plus three sedans for a week of medical-office visits. A private equity offsite in Miami needs a 56-passenger coach to the dinner venue and back. These are the trips Starter Limo Co runs as charter bus work.

The service was built as an extension of our private aviation operation. Same dispatch desk, same standards on driver vetting and vehicle quality, same account management for corporate clients. Different vehicle, different headcount, same discipline.

## How It Works

### Quote and Confirmation

Charter bus quotes typically come back within the same business day. Provide the date, headcount, pickup and drop-off locations, number of stops, and any equipment requirements (luggage capacity, ADA accessibility, on-board restroom, AV). The quote covers the coach class, hours, mileage, driver, and any add-ons. Most multi-day moves get a single rate.

### Multi-Vehicle Coordination

Most corporate group moves involve more than one vehicle class. A typical investor event runs principal sedans for executives, Sprinter shuttles for senior staff, and coaches for general attendees. Starter Limo Co assigns one account manager to the full move so the planner has one number to call, one timeline to manage, and one bill at the end.

### Driver Standards

Charter bus drivers are full-time professionals with current commercial licenses, clean DOT records, and the same training Starter Limo Co requires of black car chauffeurs. For high-profile clients and corporate events, drivers are briefed on event protocol and confidentiality before the trip starts.

### Newer Coach Equipment

The coach fleet skews newer. Late-model motorcoaches with reliable HVAC, working WiFi where contracted, clean interiors, and on-board restrooms on long-haul configurations. ADA-compliant coaches available with advance notice. For executive shuttles, conference-style seating and conference-grade AV are available on certain configurations.
