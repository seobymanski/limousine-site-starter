## The Story

### One Lincoln Town Car at Teterboro

Founder Name started Starter Limo Co in 1996 with a 1996 Black Lincoln Town Car at Teterboro. The choice of airport was not an accident. KTEB was, and is, one of the busiest private aviation fields in the world. the founder had spent enough time around its FBOs to spot a real gap in service. Charter passengers and corporate flight crews kept getting handed off to ground transportation companies built for commercial airlines. The timing was wrong. The vehicles were wrong. The drivers did not know an FBO ramp from a curbside pickup.

So he built something different. A ground transportation service that worked the way private aviation actually works. Show up before the jet does. Know the FBO. Pull onto the ramp when allowed. Stay quiet, stay early, stay ready.

### Word of Mouth, One Trip at a Time

the founder did not run ads. He ran trips. Schedulers told other schedulers. Charter brokers passed the number to their best clients. Flight departments tested the service on a Tuesday and called back on Friday. By the early 2000s, Starter Limo Co was running multiple vehicles at Teterboro and quietly expanding into Newark and JFK. By the 2010s, the company had opened a second office in South Florida at KOPF (Opa-Locka) and a third in Dallas at KDAL (Business Jet Center).

## How We Operate Today

### Three US Offices, One Standard

Starter Limo Co runs dispatch and chauffeur operations out of three US offices, each co-located with a major private aviation airport.

- **Teterboro, NJ:** 123 Main Street, at Meridian Teterboro FBO (KTEB)
- **Opa-Locka, FL:** 123 Main Street, at Fontainebleau Aviation (KOPF)
- **Dallas, TX:** 123 Main Street, at Business Jet Center (KDAL)

The same playbook runs in each office. Hire full-time chauffeurs. Train them on aviation. Pay them well enough to keep them. The result is a roster of named professionals who have driven for the same principals for years. Clients ask for Teddy, Jose L., Lenny, and Rousse by name because they have earned the seat.

### Vetted Affiliate Network

For trips outside the three US hubs, Starter Limo Co works through a vetted affiliate network covering more than 1,100 cities worldwide. The vetting is strict. Minimum fleet size, fleet age limits, chauffeur training records, insurance and licensing checks, and ongoing performance reviews. An affiliate that slips below standard does not stay an affiliate.

### Aviation-Specific Technology

Real-time flight tracking is built into dispatch. API integrations with corporate aviation systems mean schedule changes flow into our software before a phone call would have caught them. When a tail number reroutes from KTEB to KMMU, dispatch sees it. When a Gulfstream releases early at KOPF, the chauffeur is already on the ramp.

## The Industry Partnership

### Official Luxury Chauffeured Partner

Starter Limo Co is the Official Luxury Chauffeured Partner of the strategic industry partnership. our is one of the largest membership organizations for corporate flight departments and aircraft operators in the United States. The partnership exists for a simple reason. strategic partners needed a single chauffeured provider that could deliver the same standard at every field they fly into. Starter Limo Co could.

## What Has Not Changed

The fleet is newer than it was in 1996. The technology did not exist when the founder started. The geographic footprint has grown by a factor of more than a thousand. What has not changed is the focus. Private aviation, full-time chauffeurs, live dispatch, quiet service. Every trip handled like the first one.
