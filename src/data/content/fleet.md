## Pick the Right Vehicle, Not the Biggest One

Most of the time, the right vehicle is obvious to a chauffeur who has done this for years. A solo principal flying in for dinner does not need a Sprinter. A flight crew of five with bags should not be jammed into an SUV. The fleet at Starter Limo Co is built around the trips we actually run, not a parts catalog. Below is practical guidance on when each category is the right call.

## Sedan

**Best for:** Solo principal or pair of passengers, light bag count.

**Typical capacity:** Up to 3 passengers, 2 to 3 standard bags.

The sedan is the workhorse. Black, late-model, executive interior, professional chauffeur. It is the right call for the principal flying in for a meeting, the executive heading to a working dinner, or the lawyer commuting from Teterboro to a deposition in midtown. When the trip is one or two passengers and the bag situation is manageable, the sedan does the job without overcommitting on size or cost.

## SUV

**Best for:** Principal plus spouse, small family, or two executives with full luggage.

**Typical capacity:** Up to 5 passengers, 4 to 5 large bags.

The SUV is the most-requested vehicle for FBO transfers when the trip involves real luggage. Golf clubs, ski bags, weekend bags for a family of four, equipment cases for a creative shoot. The SUV also handles weather better than a sedan, which matters at fields like KASE in Aspen or KHPN in winter. When in doubt between a sedan and SUV, the SUV usually wins on comfort. Bag space tells the truth.

## Sprinter Van

**Best for:** Flight crews, executive teams, principals who want to work in transit, or trips with significant luggage.

**Typical capacity:** Up to 14 passengers depending on configuration, plus large bag capacity.

The Sprinter is the right answer for crew rotations, road shows, and any trip where the passengers want privacy and room to work. Executive Sprinter configurations include captain's chairs, conference seating, and divider partitions. Production crews and security teams use the Sprinter as a mobile office, with bags stowed separately and the cabin kept quiet. For private aviation clients, the Sprinter solves the simultaneous-arrival problem: principal in an SUV, crew and bags in the Sprinter, both leaving the FBO at the same time.

## Coach (Charter Bus)

**Best for:** Corporate events, conferences, road shows, large group movements.

**Typical capacity:** 30 to 56 passengers depending on coach class.

The coach is what you call when the group is too large for Sprinters. Newer motorcoach equipment with ADA-compliant configurations available, professional drivers with clean DOT records, and the same dispatch backbone that runs our black car operation. Common use cases include conference shuttles between hotel and venue, corporate offsites, sports team transport, and multi-stop tours. Coaches can be reserved as part of a multi-vehicle package alongside sedans and SUVs, with one account manager coordinating the full move.
